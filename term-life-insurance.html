At a glance

Term insurance is a life insurance protection product. If the insured event occurs during the covered period and the claim is admissible under the policy terms, the benefit is paid according to the contract. It should be evaluated as protection rather than as a promised investment return.

Why term insurance is used

The main purpose is to create financial protection for dependants if the insured person dies during the policy period. The required cover is usually considered in the context of family expenses, outstanding liabilities, future goals and existing financial resources.

Sum assured and policy term

The sum assured is the contractual life cover, while the policy term is the duration of coverage. A higher cover or longer term may increase premium and is subject to underwriting.

Medical and financial underwriting

Insurers may ask about health, occupation, lifestyle, income and existing insurance. Depending on the application, medical tests or additional documents may be required before the insurer accepts, modifies or declines the proposal.

Riders and optional features

Some policies offer optional riders or benefits for specified events. Each rider has separate definitions, exclusions and cost, so it should be reviewed on its own merit rather than automatically added.

Disclosure matters

Accurate disclosure of health, smoking/tobacco use, occupation, existing policies and other material information is important. The policy document should be checked promptly after issuance and nominee details kept current.

Frequently asked questions

What is term life insurance?

Term life insurance is a protection-oriented life insurance policy that provides life cover for a defined period according to the policy contract.

Is term insurance an investment product?

Its primary purpose is life protection, not investment return. Product variants can differ, so the exact policy wording should be checked.

How much term cover is enough?

There is no single amount for everyone. Dependants, income, liabilities, future goals, existing assets and existing insurance should be considered.

What is the policy term?

The policy term is the duration for which the life cover is provided, subject to premium payment and other policy conditions.

Why does the insurer ask for medical details?

Health and lifestyle information is used for underwriting. The insurer may request medical tests or additional information before deciding the proposal.

Why is accurate disclosure important?

Insurance contracts rely on material information provided in the proposal. Incorrect or incomplete disclosure can affect underwriting and future claims.

What is a nominee in life insurance?

A nominee is the person nominated to receive policy proceeds in accordance with applicable law and policy conditions. Nomination details should be kept updated.

What are riders?

Riders are optional benefits attached to the base policy for specified risks or events. They come with separate terms, exclusions and additional cost where applicable.

Is a term insurance claim always paid?

Claims are evaluated according to the policy wording, disclosures, exclusions, documentation and applicable law. No claim should be assumed to be automatic.

What should I compare before choosing term insurance?

Compare cover amount, policy term, premium structure, insurer service, exclusions, underwriting requirements, claim process and relevant optional benefits.
