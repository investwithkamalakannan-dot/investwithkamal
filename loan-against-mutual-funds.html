Loan Against Mutual Funds (LAMF) in Coimbatore

LAMF can provide liquidity by creating a lien or pledge on eligible mutual fund units instead of immediately redeeming them, subject to lender rules and eligibility.

At a glance

A loan against mutual funds is a secured credit facility in which eligible mutual fund units are marked under a lien or pledge in favour of a lender. The lender decides eligible schemes, loan value, margin, interest, repayment and documentation. Market movements can affect drawing power and collateral requirements.

How LAMF works

Eligible mutual fund units are identified and a lien or pledge is created according to the lender and fund/depository process. The lender then sanctions a credit limit or loan based on its valuation and policy.

Eligibility and loan value

Not every mutual fund scheme or folio is necessarily eligible. The lender can apply different loan-to-value or margin requirements depending on the type of mutual fund units and its own credit policy.

What happens to pledged units

While units are under lien or pledge, redemption or other transactions can be restricted until the lender releases the lien. The exact rights and process depend on the facility documents and the way the units are held.

Market and margin risk

Because mutual fund values can change, the collateral value can fall. A lender may require additional margin, reduce the available limit or take other action under the loan agreement if the collateral no longer meets its requirements.

When to compare LAMF with redemption

LAMF creates borrowing cost and repayment obligations. Compare the interest, charges, loan period and risk of keeping the investment pledged against the consequences of redeeming investments. Liquidity should not be borrowed without understanding repayment capacity.

Frequently asked questions

What is a loan against mutual funds?

It is a secured loan or credit facility where eligible mutual fund units are pledged or lien-marked in favour of the lender.

Do I have to sell my mutual funds to take LAMF?

Generally, eligible units are pledged or lien-marked rather than redeemed at the start, subject to the lender’s process.

Are all mutual funds eligible for LAMF?

No. Eligibility is decided by the lender and can differ by scheme category, holding mode and other criteria.

How is the loan amount decided?

The lender usually applies its valuation, loan-to-value or margin policy to eligible units. The available limit can change if the market value changes.

Can I redeem pledged mutual fund units?

Redemption is generally restricted while the lien or pledge is active unless the lender permits release according to the facility terms.

What happens if mutual fund values fall?

A fall in collateral value may reduce drawing power or trigger a requirement for additional margin or repayment under the lender’s terms.

Is LAMF interest-free?

No. It is a borrowing facility and interest and other charges can apply according to the lender’s schedule.

Is LAMF better than redeeming mutual funds?

There is no universal answer. Compare borrowing cost, repayment capacity, taxes, investment purpose, liquidity need and the consequences of keeping units pledged.

Who approves a loan against mutual funds?

The lender makes the credit decision and sets eligibility, documentation, valuation, margin and repayment conditions.

What should I read before taking LAMF?

Read the lender’s sanction terms, interest and charge schedule, margin rules, lien/pledge process, repayment terms and consequences of collateral value changes.
